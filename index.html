What to check before ordering

Shoes

Measure both feet and compare the longer measurement with the seller’s size chart. In warehouse QC photos, check the size label, outsole and both sides of the pair.

Hoodies and T-shirts

Lay a garment you already own flat and compare chest width, body length and sleeve length. Check whether the seller lists flat width or full circumference.

Jackets

Measure over the layers you expect to wear. Check the zipper, lining and cuffs in warehouse photos. Ask for a close-up if a detail is unclear.

Shopping questions

How do I use a product link with Sugargoo?

Open the HipobuyFind item and look for Sugargoo in its buying options. If you use the original seller URL, confirm that the item, size and color shown by the agent match the seller’s listing before paying.

What goes into the delivered price?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, any service fees, international shipping and any applicable import charges. Check whether your route bills on actual or volumetric weight.

When should I review QC photos?

Review the warehouse quality-control photos before approving international shipping. If the item differs from your order, check the seller and agent’s return deadlines before requesting a return or exchange.
